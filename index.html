The shortlist

AI names three businesses. Are you one of them?

Ask ChatGPT or Google’s AI who to hire and you don’t get ten links to compare. You get a short list of names and a reason for each. The businesses on that list get the call. The rest may never be considered.

How it works

From free check to proof in 45 days.

One free first step, one fixed sprint, one re-check on the same questions. You decide at every step whether to go on.

  1. Free · 2 minutes

    1Free AI check

    3 real questions about your business, answered live by ChatGPT. No email needed.

    Run it now

  2. Free

    2Free audit and 45-day roadmap

    A report that’s yours to keep: where AI names you, who it names instead and what to fix first.

  3. 15 minutes

    3A short call

    We walk you through the roadmap. Do it yourself, or with us.

  4. 45 days

    445-day sprint

    Fixed scope, a shared tracker you can open any time, a weekly update by email.

  5. Day 45

    5Re-check on the same questions

    Same questions, same engines, next to day 0. Then a monthly plan, or keep going on your own.

What the sprint works on

01

Get understood

AI can’t recommend a business it can’t read.

  • Website fixes: AI crawler access, schema, indexing
  • The same name, address, phone and facts everywhere
  • Google Business Profile or company facts page fixed up

02

Get cited

AI repeats what trusted sites say about you.

  • Directory and review-site listings set up or fixed
  • Review platforms like G2, Capterra or Trustpilot
  • PR and mention outreach, counted in pitches

03

Get recommended

Give AI a page that answers the question.

  • Service and area pages for local businesses
  • “X vs Y”, alternatives and “best for” pages for online businesses
  • An FAQ written for AI answers

04

Get chosen

Turn the recommendation into a call or a booking.

  • A review routine: request and reply templates, QR card
  • Lead capture: click-to-call, quote form, booking link
  • Call, form and AI-referral tracking

FAQ

Questions before you buy

Run a free AI check
What is AI search optimization, and how is it different from SEO?

AI search optimization is the work that gets your business named when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google’s AI who to choose. SEO gets a page ranked in a list of links. An AI answer names a few businesses and says why, so the work also covers what other sites say about you: listings, reviews and mentions. Good SEO still helps, because AI tools read the web through search.

Which AI tools do you check?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. Online and national plans add Perplexity, because buyers use it for research. Every plan lists how many questions we re-check and on which engines.

Can you guarantee I’ll be recommended?

No. AI answers change daily and no one controls them. What we do guarantee is the work: every deliverable listed in your plan ships within the timeline, and on day 45 you see the same questions on the same engines next to the day-0 answers.

What happens if not much changes by day 45?

You still get every listed deliverable, plus a day-45 report that shows what changed, what didn’t and the likely reasons. We tell you what we would do next and whether a monthly plan is worth it for you. If it isn’t, we say so.

Do I need a monthly plan after the sprint?

No. The sprint is one-off and everything it builds stays yours. A monthly plan makes sense if you want a re-check every month, new pages each month and help keeping reviews and listings up to date. Monthly plans start after a sprint, or after our free audit confirms your basics are already in place.

Who owns the pages, listings and accounts?

You do, from day one. We work in your accounts with the access you give us, and you can remove it at any time. Pages, listings, reports and tracking stay with you if you stop working with us.

Do I approve the content before it goes live?

Yes. You sign off the deliverables list before work starts, and you approve every new page and listing text before we publish it.

What do you need from me?

Access to your website, your Google Business Profile (for local plans) and your analytics, and about 2 hours of your time over the 45 days: a kickoff, a few approvals and the day-45 review.

How do I pay?

Sprints are paid once: upfront, or 50/50. Monthly plans are billed monthly and you can cancel any month. You get a fixed price in writing before you pay anything.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. We work with businesses in the US, the UK and the EU, and quote in dollars, pounds or euros.
