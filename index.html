Free Report and Roadmap
What is AI search optimization?

It's the work of getting your brand named, cited and recommended when buyers ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews. You'll also hear it called GEO (generative engine optimization) or AEO (answer engine optimization).

How is this different from SEO?

SEO ranks you in a list of links. AI search gets you named inside the answer. Both need a fast, crawlable site and content worth citing, but AI also weighs what the rest of the web says about you: reviews, roundups, communities and media. We work on both, because Google's AI answers still lean on organic rankings.

You say you bring leads, not just visibility. How?

We treat AI visibility as the top of the funnel. Every page AI sends buyers to gets built and tested for conversion: message match with the AI's answer, proof, pricing cues, short forms and instant booking. Then we track AI-referred visitors through to demos, pipeline and closed deals in your CRM.

How do you track leads that come from AI?

Three ways together: referral and landing page data in GA4, source fields carried into your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive), and a “How did you hear about us?” field, because many buyers read the AI answer and come back later through search or direct.

How fast will we see results?

Technical and on-site fixes can show up in AI answers within weeks, especially on platforms that search the live web. Authority work compounds over three to six months. Conversion gains on AI traffic start as soon as new landing pages go live.

Do you only work with B2B and SaaS companies?

Yes. Long sales cycles, buying committees and comparison-heavy research are where AI recommendations matter most, and where our playbooks come from.

What's in the free report and roadmap?

Where AI recommends you and where it names competitors instead, across 100+ prompts your buyers actually ask. Your share of voice by platform. The conversion leaks on pages AI sends buyers to. And a 90-day roadmap ranked by pipeline impact. It's yours to keep, whether we work together or not.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No one honestly can, because AI answers change with every model update. What we commit to: a fixed prompt set measured every month, clear targets for visibility and pipeline, and a plan that changes when the data says so.
