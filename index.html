The platform

People + AI agents, working as one team.

AI agents do the volume: research, checks, first drafts and monitoring. People make the calls: strategy, editing, outreach and anything published under your name.

AI agents do the volume

  • 01 · Learns your business

    Context agent

    Builds one fact base from your site, your profiles and your answers: services, areas, prices, proof and tone of voice. Every page and listing draws from it, so nothing reads like a template.

  • 02 · Finds high-intent questions

    Research agent

    Collects the questions, comparisons, locations and use cases buyers search in Google and ask AI before they contact anyone, then ranks them by how close they are to a purchase.

  • 03 · Re-asks the AI engines

    Answer-check agent

    Puts those questions to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI on a schedule and records who is named, in what order and which sources are cited.

  • 04 · Drafts pages that sell

    Content agent

    Drafts service, location, comparison, cost and FAQ pages from the fact base. Each one is built around a single buyer question and a single next step.

  • 05 · Maps where trust comes from

    Authority agent

    Finds the directories, review sites and publications AI cites in your category and prepares the listings, fixes and pitches that put you there.

  • 06 · Follows the lead

    Conversion agent

    Checks every page for a clear path to a call, a form or a booking, filters out spam and records which pages each lead read before reaching out.

People make the calls

  • Your account lead

What is organic lead generation in the AI search era?

It is getting enquiries from people who search Google or ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI who to choose, without paying for each click. The work is the same in both places: answer the questions buyers ask right before they choose, give AI and Google proof they can trust, and make every page end in a call, a form or a booking.

How is this different from SEO?

Classic SEO chases rankings and traffic. We start from buying intent: the questions people ask right before they contact someone, such as “best … in …”, “… vs …” or “how much does … cost”. An AI answer also names a few businesses and says why, so the work covers what other sites say about you: listings, reviews and mentions. Good SEO still helps, because AI tools read the web through search.

Which solution should I start with?

Most businesses start with the AI Search Sprint: 45 days that fix the base on your website and on the outside sources AI reads. AI Search Monthly keeps that base growing. Pages adds a hub of conversion pages when buyers ask far more questions than your site answers. The free audit tells you which one fits, and it is fine to run them together.

What are organic conversion pages?

Pages built for one high-intent search each: a service in a location, a comparison, a cost question. They are written to rank in Google and be quoted in AI answers, and each one ends in a clear next step. Our Pages solution builds a hub of them on your domain.

What do the AI agents do, and what do people do?

AI agents do the volume: research into what buyers ask, scheduled checks of AI answers, first drafts of pages, monitoring and lead tracking. People make the decisions: strategy, editing and fact-checking, outreach to real editors and review sites, and anything published under your name. You approve pages before they go live.

Which AI tools do you check?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. National and international work adds Perplexity, because buyers use it for research. Every scope lists how many questions we re-check and on which engines.

Can you guarantee I’ll be recommended, or a number of leads?

No. AI answers change daily and no one controls them. What we do guarantee is the work: every deliverable on your list ships on time, you see the same questions re-asked on the same engines, and you see which pages your enquiries came from.

How soon will I see results?

The sprint shows a before and after on day 45. Leads from new pages take longer: most businesses should expect the first enquiries from a hub of pages around month 3 or 4, and more from there. It depends on your market, your competition and how strong your site is today.

Who owns the pages, listings and accounts?

You do, from day one. We work in your accounts with the access you give us, and you can remove it at any time. Pages, listings, reports and tracking stay with you if you stop working with us.

How does pricing work?

Each solution has a starting price on its own page, in dollars, euros or pounds. You get the exact scope and a fixed price in writing before you pay. The sprint is paid once, upfront or 50/50. Monthly work and Pages are billed monthly and you can cancel any month.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. We work with local, national and international businesses in the US, the UK and the EU, and quote in dollars, pounds or euros.
