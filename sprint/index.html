What the sprint works on

Four jobs, in the order AI and buyers need them.

Two of them happen on your website, one on the outside sources AI reads, and the last one wherever a buyer can reach you.

01 · On your website

Get understood

  • Website fixes: AI crawler access, schema, indexing
  • The same name, address, phone and facts everywhere
  • Google Business Profile or company facts page fixed up

02 · On outside sources

Get cited

AI repeats what trusted sites say about you.

  • Directory and review-site listings set up or fixed

Get recommended

  • A review routine: request and reply templates, QR card
  • Call, form and AI-referral tracking

What does the starting price cover, and what raises it?

The starting price covers the scope listed on this page: one location and up to three services for a local business, or one product or service line in one market for a national one. More locations, services, markets, languages, pages or outreach raise the price. You get the exact deliverables list and the price in writing before you pay.

Can you guarantee I’ll be recommended by day 45?

No. AI answers change daily and no one controls them. What we do guarantee is the work: every deliverable on your list ships within the 45 days, and on day 45 you see the same questions on the same engines next to the day-0 answers.

What happens if not much changes by day 45?

You still get every listed deliverable, plus a report that shows what changed, what didn’t and the likely reasons. We tell you what we would do next and whether monthly work is worth it for you. If it isn’t, we say so.

Do I need a monthly plan after the sprint?

No. The sprint is one-off and everything it builds stays yours. Monthly work makes sense if you want a re-check every month, new pages and help keeping reviews and listings current. Pages makes sense if you want a hub that covers many more buying questions.

What do you need from me?

Access to your website, your Google Business Profile (for local businesses) and your analytics, and about 2 hours of your time over the 45 days: a kickoff, a few approvals and the day-45 review.

How do I pay?

Once: upfront, or 50/50. You get a fixed price in writing before you pay anything.
