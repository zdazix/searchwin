Do I need a sprint before monthly work?

Monthly work starts after a sprint, or after our free audit confirms your basics are already in place. Monthly work adds to a base. If AI crawlers can’t read your site or your facts differ from one listing to the next, the sprint fixes that first.

What does the starting price cover, and what raises it?

The starting price covers the monthly scope listed on this page. More questions and engines, more pages a month, more locations or markets and outreach for mentions raise the price. You get the exact monthly scope and price in writing before you pay.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any month. There is no minimum term, and every page, listing and account stays yours.

How is this different from Pages?

Monthly works on the website and profiles you already have, with a few new or refreshed pages a month. Pages builds a separate hub of conversion pages on your domain, at volume. Monthly keeps the base strong; Pages widens how many buying questions you answer.

How do you report leads?

Each month you get calls, forms and bookings by source and by page, where they can be tracked. We set up call and form tracking in the sprint, or in the first month if you start without one.

Can you guarantee a number of leads?

No. What we do guarantee is the work: the counted list for the month ships, and the report shows what it changed in the answers and in your enquiries.
