Why a hub

Buyers ask hundreds of specific questions. Most websites answer a handful.

Being found is half the job. The page a buyer lands on has to answer the question, earn trust and make the next step obvious.

  1. 01

    They research before they call

    Comparisons, costs, locations, use cases: buyers search and ask AI about all of it before they contact a vendor.

  2. 02

    AI answers from pages

    When no page of yours answers the question, AI quotes someone else’s. A hub gives it your answer, your proof and your name.

  3. 03

    Traffic is not the goal

    Pages are picked by buying intent and built to end in an enquiry. We report leads by page, not visits.

Page types

Built for how buyers search, compare and decide.

Different questions need different pages. The hub mixes them to match your market.

  • Service pages

    One page per service, capability, industry or use case buyers search for.

  • Location pages

    Your service in each town, region or country you sell in.

  • Comparison pages

    “X vs Y” and “best for” pages that help buyers weigh options, methods and vendors.

  • Alternatives pages

    For buyers who know a competitor’s name and want to see who else there is.

  • Cost and pricing pages

    Honest price ranges and what drives them, for people close to a decision.

  • Category pages

    Structured pages for product groups, equipment types and catalog sections.

  • Guides and FAQs

    Short answers to the questions buyers ask before, during and after choosing.

  • Visuals

    Branded images, tables and diagrams that make a page easier to scan and to quote.

What are organic conversion pages?

Pages built for one high-intent search each, such as a service in a location, a comparison or a cost question. They are written to rank in Google and be quoted in AI answers, and every one ends in a clear next step: a call, a form or a booking.

Is this the same as SEO?

No. Classic SEO usually chases rankings and traffic. Pages starts from buying intent: we pick the searches people make right before they contact a vendor and build pages that turn those searches into enquiries.

Is the content written by AI?

AI agents do the research and the first draft from a fact base about your business. A person edits and fact-checks every page before it goes live, and you can approve each one. For technical or regulated niches a specialist reviews the page as well.

Where do the pages live?

On your domain, in a folder such as yourbusiness.com/guides/ or on a subdomain, in your design. You own every page, and they stay with you if you stop.

Do I need to write briefs or manage a content calendar?

No. We handle research, planning, writing, publishing, links and tracking. After a short onboarding you spend a few minutes a week reviewing progress and approving pages.

How soon will I see leads?

The hub is live in under 30 days. Most businesses should expect the first enquiries from it around month 3 or 4, and more from there. It depends on your market, your competition and how strong your site is today, and we don’t promise a number.

What is included at the starting price, and what changes it?

Everything on this page: setup, research, writing, publishing, links, tracking and support, with every agent switched on. The final price depends on how many pages you want published, how fast you want to grow and how much outreach you add.

Do I need the AI Search Sprint first?

Not always. If AI crawlers can read your site and your facts are consistent, Pages can start on its own. If the base is broken, the free audit says so and we recommend the sprint first, or both together.

How is success measured?

By qualified enquiries: calls, forms and bookings by page. Under that we report positions in Google, mentions in AI answers, AI crawler visits and how each page performs.
