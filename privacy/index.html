Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what SearchWin.ai ("we", "us") collects when you visit searchwin.ai, run the free AI visibility check or ask for a full audit, why we collect it, who we share it with and how to have it deleted. Questions: hi@searchwin.ai.

What we collect

  • The website you enter in the free check, and public information we read from that website: business name, services, city, state, phone numbers, addresses and email addresses published on the site.
  • The results of the check: the questions we ask ChatGPT, ChatGPT's answers, the businesses and sources it names. When a check finishes we keep a record of it: the website you entered, the business details we read from it, the answers we received and the result (how many answers named you, and your best position), even if you do not leave an email. If you ask for the full audit, the same data is stored with your email request.
  • Your email address, only if you ask for the full audit (or leave your website and email in the form for online businesses), together with the text of the consent you agreed to.
  • A call booking, if you choose to book one on the thank-you page: the scheduling page is provided by a scheduling service, and we pass your email address to it so the form is pre-filled. What you enter there is handled by that service.
  • Technical data saved with an audit request: browser and device (user agent), the page you landed on, the referring page, and advertising parameters in the link you clicked (UTM tags, Google click IDs gclid/gbraid/wbraid, Meta click ID fbclid). Our hosting provider and Cloudflare process your IP address to deliver the page and check for bots; we do not store it.
  • Cookies, listed below.

How we use it

  • To run the check you asked for and show you the result.
  • To prepare and email you the full audit and 45-day roadmap you asked for, and to follow up about it.
  • To send you occasional emails about AI search and our services. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To protect the free check from abuse (bot protection).
  • To measure our advertising and show our ads to people who visited the check (see below).

Advertising pixels and hashed emails

The free check page uses the Meta Pixel and Google tags for Google Ads and Google Analytics 4. They run in your browser and record that you visited the page, saw a result or asked for an audit, so that Meta and Google can measure our ads and build audiences for them, including showing our ads to people who saw a result but did not ask for an audit. Like any page request, these tags send Meta and Google your IP address and browser details.

When you ask for the full audit, the tags send Meta and Google a SHA-256 hash of your email address, made in your browser before it is sent. A hash is a one-way code: Meta and Google use it to match the event to an account they already know, not to read your email. You can control ad personalisation in your Meta ad preferences and Google ad settings.

Who we share data with

  • Vercel (hosting) and Neon (database), where the site and data are stored.
  • DataForSEO, which sends our questions to ChatGPT and returns the answers. It receives the questions, not your email.
  • Google (Gemini API), which reads the public text of the website you entered to find the business name, service and city.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile bot protection).
  • Meta and Google, as described above.
  • Telegram, which carries internal notifications to our team about new audit requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Records of finished checks without an email: no longer than 24 months.
  • Audit requests and your email: until you ask us to delete them, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you.

Your choices and how to delete your data

Email hi@searchwin.ai from the address you gave us to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop all emails. We reply within 30 days. You can also unsubscribe with the link in any email. If you live in California or another US state, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or any other place with a privacy law, you have the rights that law gives you, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Children

The check is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced on this page before they take effect.
